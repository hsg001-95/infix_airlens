## CPCB AQI Categories and Health Impact
- **Good (0–50):** Minimal impact. Safe for all outdoor activities.
- **Satisfactory (51–100):** Minor breathing discomfort to sensitive people.
- **Moderate (101–200):** Breathing discomfort to people with asthma, heart disease, children, and older adults.
- **Poor (201–300):** Breathing discomfort to most people on prolonged exposure. Sensitive groups should reduce prolonged or heavy outdoor exertion.
- **Very Poor (301–400):** Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure. Everyone should avoid prolonged outdoor exertion; children, elderly, and people with heart or lung disease should stay indoors and keep activity levels low.
- **Severe (401–500):** Affects healthy people and seriously impacts those with existing respiratory or cardiovascular diseases. Avoid all outdoor physical activity; keep windows and doors sealed during peak inversion hours.
