## Safe Indoor Air Practices & Blocklist
- **Masks:** Cloth and surgical masks provide minimal filtration against fine PM2.5 particles. A well-fitted N95 or FFP2 respirator without an exhalation valve filters ≥95% of airborne particles when sealed tightly over the nose bridge and chin. Not recommended for children under 2 or individuals experiencing breathing difficulty.
- **Carbon Monoxide Warning:** NEVER seal windows and doors tightly while burning coal, charcoal (angithi/sigri), wood, kerosene, or unvented gas heaters indoors—this causes life-threatening carbon monoxide buildup.
- **Blocked / Unsupported Claims:** Do not use ozone-generating "air purifiers" (ozone irritates lungs). Houseplants do not meaningfully reduce indoor PM2.5 at room scale.
- **Purifiers & DIY Filter Boxes:** Size HEPA purifiers by Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR) for the bedroom volume and avoid active ionizer modes. For DIY box-fan + MERV-13 filter cubes, use only certified fans with thermal overload protection and never leave unattended near young children.
