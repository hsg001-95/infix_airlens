## Delhi-NCR GRAP Stages and Citizen Guidance
- **Stage I – Poor (AQI 201–300):** Strict ban on open burning of biomass and municipal solid waste. Citizens should keep vehicle engines tuned, check PUC certificates, and avoid idling at red lights.
- **Stage II – Very Poor (AQI 301–400):** Enhanced parking fees to discourage private vehicles; augmented Metro and CNG/electric bus frequency. Citizens are advised to use public transport, carpool, work from home where feasible, and provide electric heaters to security/sanitation staff so they do not burn wood or waste.
- **Stage III – Severe (AQI 401–450):** Strict restrictions on dust-generating construction/demolition and BS-III petrol / BS-IV diesel four-wheelers in NCR. Hybrid/online classes for primary schools where notified; offices advised to adopt 50% work-from-home.
- **Stage IV – Severe+ (AQI > 450):** Emergency ban on non-essential diesel trucks entering Delhi and linear public construction. Children, elderly, and those with respiratory or cardiac conditions should remain strictly indoors.
